What Is PDC, Really?

A Danish software company that builds the "brain" behind scheduling in airports, hospitals, and more.

What PDC Does — It makes software that helps plan who works when, which flight lands when, and which gate to use. Think of it as a super-smart calendar that handles complex rules automatically.
Born with AI — PDC created Visual Prolog, a programming language designed for logical thinking and rule-based problem solving. This has been their secret weapon from day one.
Not Just a Dashboard — When a plan breaks (someone calls in sick, a flight gets delayed), PDC's engine figures out what to do next and suggests fixes in real time.
Bonus: Sister Companies — Dictus does speech recognition (talk to the system instead of clicking), and ARGOS helps with chemical and nuclear emergency response.

What's Hard About Scheduling?

Three big headaches that make scheduling way more difficult than it sounds.

Things Break All the Time

Bad weather, sick staff, equipment failure — a schedule that works at 8 AM can be useless by 9 AM. The longer it takes to fix, the more money and safety are at risk.

For example: a delayed flight means the crew can't make the next one, the gate is occupied, and connecting passengers miss their flights.

Real-Time

Too Many Rules

Every industry has a mountain of rules: flight-time limits, union contracts, airport slot rules, response-time requirements. PDC's engine handles them, but keeping all those rules updated is still a lot of manual work.

The gap: the system is smart, but someone still has to feed it new rules by hand.

Complexity

Hard to Learn

PDC's software is powerful — but it takes years of practice to master. New dispatchers and managers face a steep learning curve, and every minute spent clicking through menus is a minute not solving real problems.

The missed opportunity: the power is there, but it's locked behind a complex interface.

My Proposed AI Solutions

Four AI agents that build on top of PDC's existing engine — not replace it.

Auto-Fix Agent

Detect · Think · Fix

Watches the system in real time. When something goes wrong (delay, cancellation), it automatically checks all the rules and suggests the best fix. The human dispatcher just reviews and clicks "approve."

Something Breaks Check Rules Suggest Fix Human Says Yes
  • Cuts fix time from ~30 min to ~2 min
  • Always follows the rules — no shortcuts
  • Gets smarter over time by learning from human choices
Biggest Impact

Crystal Ball Agent

Predict · Prepare · Optimize

Studies past patterns — when delays usually happen, how many emergency calls come in on a Monday, how many shoppers show up at 3 PM. Then it helps plan ahead so problems get prevented instead of just fixed.

Past Data Make Prediction Adjust Plan Early Problem Avoided
  • Changes the game from reactive to proactive
  • Less overtime, fewer last-minute panics
  • Keeps improving as it sees more data
Predictive

Team Negotiator

Balance · Trade-Off · Agree

When different teams want different things — crew needs rest, but the airline wants the flight to depart — multiple mini-agents each argue for their team, and a "judge" agent finds the best overall compromise.

Conflict! Crew Agent Fleet Agent Ops Agent Best Deal
  • Like real meetings — but in seconds, not hours
  • Finds solutions humans might miss
  • Works naturally with PDC's multi-industry setup
Most Creative

Chat Assistant

Talk · Understand · Do

Instead of clicking through complex menus, users just type or speak what they want. The AI understands the request, checks the rules behind the scenes, and gets it done. Powered by Dictus's speech tech.

"Move Jane to Monday" Understand Intent Check Rules "Done. One issue..."
  • Anyone can use it — no more months of training
  • Works with voice commands (Dictus tech)
  • Unlocks PDC's power for non-expert users
